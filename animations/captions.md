# Captions for animations
Captions for animations S1-S10 are provided here. As the visualisation criteria for the cell-based composition field is the same for all animations, the detailed explanation is only provided in the caption of animation S1.

### Animation S1
Temporal evolution of the quadrant annulus model *QDf01r2* with diffusion creep rheology, friction coefficient of 0.1 and initial mantle potential temperature of 1900 K. The four quadrants represent different fields, counter-clockwise from top: viscosity, temperature, composition and strain rate. In the temperature field, white lines are isotherms of 1000, 2000, 3000 K. The cell-based composition field represents the following different material: TTG (>= 60% in tangerine), basalt (>= 60% in dark purple), harzburgite (>= 40% in teal, lighter shades represent higher harzburgite content and mantle depletion, TTG-melt (>= 50% in peach-orange), basaltic-melt (>= 50% in sky blue), harzburgitic-melt (>= 50% in white), and TTG-basalt-mix (>= 40% TTG and >= 40% basalt in light purple). The empty cells in black represent mixes of different materials that do not fit either of the above criteria.
